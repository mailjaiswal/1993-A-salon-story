The Legend of 1993 A Salon Company

1993-SALON-PASS EXCLUSIVITY GUARANTEED
VERIFIED

“She believed she could, so she did.”

1993 saw the rise of a new star in the highly competitive salon & spa industry when Mrs. Nayana Chopade set up Salon Apple in Pune offering only the best of salon services with a small team of experts under her watchful eye.

In a span of 10 years, Salon Apple became synonymous with quality and consistency, encouraging our founder to extend her passion into empowering youth through Fillit Institute of Hair & Beauty Management.

Today, 1993 A Salon Company under Apple Hair & Beauty Services Pvt. Ltd. operates luxury salon outlets at Prabhat Road, DP Road, Magarpatta, Dange Chowk, Salunkevihar in Pune, and Pandharpur, rapidly expanding Pan-India.
